Our Approach to Fundraising Compliance

As a private #deeptech venture advancing proprietary CTPSci research into fundamental gravity and energy domains, we are committed to conducting our fundraising efforts in full compliance with applicable securities laws.

We are relying on Rule 506(c) of Regulation D under the Securities Act of 1933. This exemption:

  • Permits general solicitation and general advertising (including public posts on LinkedIn, X, and other platforms)
  • Restricts participation exclusively to verified accredited investors
  • Requires the issuer to take reasonable steps to verify accredited investor status

In line with current SEC guidance (including the March 2025 no-action letter providing a streamlined verification framework for Rule 506(c)), our verification process will include:

  • A minimum investment threshold of $200,000 for natural persons / $1,000,000 for entities (or higher)
  • Written representations from investors confirming accredited status and that funds are not third-party funds
  • No acceptance of funds absent reasonable verification steps and absent red flags

We do not accept investments from non-accredited investors under this offering.

Fact sheet · As of March 31, 2026 · 2 pages

Tuttle Capital UFO Disclosure ETF — Fact Sheet

The Tuttle Capital UFO Disclosure ETF (UFOD) is designed to invest in companies positioned to benefit from government disclosure, confirmation, or exploitation of advanced technologies tied to unidentified anomalous phenomena. As the fact sheet puts it: “Disclosure Is an Economic Event.”

The fund is weighted toward companies at the intersection of aerospace, defense, advanced materials, sensing, and energy — the industries poised to receive a massive influx of capital and technology in the event of a UFO or UAP disclosure.

Key facts: Ticker UFOD · Listed on Cboe BZX Exchange, Inc. · Inception date February 5, 2026 · Gross expense ratio 0.99% · Net assets $1.64M (as of March 31, 2026) · Shares outstanding 70,000 · CUSIP 26923W298 · Fund advisor Tuttle Capital Management · Distributor Foreside Fund Services.

Largest holdings (as of March 31, 2026): Amentum Holdings Inc (AMTM) 4.96%, Lockheed Martin Corp (LMT) 4.91%, Northrop Grumman Corp (NOC) 3.50%, DuPont de Nemours Inc (DD) 3.31%, L3Harris Technologies Inc (LHX) 3.10%, Palantir Technologies Inc (PLTR) 3.06%, Emerson Electric Co (EMR) 2.80%, Leidos Holdings Inc (LDOS) 2.66%, GE Vernova Inc (GEV) 2.61%, ATI Inc (ATI) 2.49%.

The fact sheet’s own risk section notes the theme is highly speculative: government confirmation or denial of advanced alien technology is uncertain, and rumored breakthroughs might never materialize. Past performance is no guarantee of future results.

Prospectus (Form 485B) · February 2, 2026 · 16 pages

Tuttle Capital UFO Disclosure ETF — Prospectus (Form 485B)

The prospectus describes a fund that seeks capital appreciation by investing at least 80% of its net assets in companies the Adviser believes will benefit from UFO Disclosure — the disclosure, confirmation, or exploitation of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) and Non-Human Intelligence (NHI) technologies. The strategy is thematic and, in the prospectus’s own words, “based on the Adviser’s beliefs and opinions and is entirely speculative.”

The prospectus states that no governmental authority has confirmed the existence of NHI, NHI-origin technology, or the successful reverse engineering of NHI technologies, and that to date most UAP investigations resolve to mundane explanations. It also states there is no guarantee the fund’s investment strategy will be properly implemented, and an investor may lose some or all of its investment.

Key facts: Ticker UFOD · Listed on Cboe BZX Exchange, Inc. · Investment objective: capital appreciation · Management fee 0.99% · Total annual fund operating expenses 0.99% · Adviser: Tuttle Capital Management, LLC · Portfolio manager: Matthew Tuttle, Chief Executive Officer of the Adviser (since inception in 2025) · Non-diversified · Shares issued in Creation Units of at least 10,000 shares; individual shares trade on the secondary market.
