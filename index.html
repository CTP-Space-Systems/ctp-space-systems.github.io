Vision / About

CTP Space Systems™: Pioneering Humanity’s Next Frontier.

At CTP Space Systems, we are the rightful continuation of Nikola Tesla’s lineage and legacy, advancing his visionary principles for the 21st century. Guided by the emerging science of CTP Energy, our mission is to unlock the same technologies and principles that UFOs and UAPs have demonstrated for decades—bringing them into the realm of human achievement.

Our revolutionary CTP spacecraft leverage CTP Energy to actively control and modulate gravity, enabling CTP Gravitic Propulsion™. Unlike chemical rockets, which are limited by fuel, trajectory, and extreme g-forces, CTP Gravitic Propulsion allows for:

  • Instantaneous and precise maneuvering in any direction, free from inertia limitations
  • Extreme energy efficiency with minimal environmental impact
  • Safe, long-duration interstellar travel without the constraints of mass ratios or orbital mechanics
  • Stealth and resilience unmatched by conventional propulsion systems

By harnessing these advanced capabilities, we are not just building spacecraft—we are building humanity’s interstellar future. Our work translates the non-physical sciences behind observed UFO phenomena into practical, scalable, and repeatable engineering for human exploration.

We are laying the foundation for a new era of space travel where interstellar missions, colonization, and advanced scientific exploration become achievable within human lifetimes.

CTP Energy and the post-quantum/post-physics CTPSci Sciences are the key. CTP Space Systems is the vanguard of the next great leap in human capability.

Your long-awaited Disclosure is finally here in the emergence of a new science we call CTP. Join us as we redefine what’s possible and lead humanity toward a future among the stars that is possible in our lifetime.

Advancing human consciousness in order to achieve interstellar travel within the next 10–15 years. We say it’s possible. Let’s do it!
